# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The maintainers and contributors of review want everyone to take part in this project without harassment. This applies to every person, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience level, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Expected behavior

These behaviors help to keep the project a good place to work:

- Be respectful and kind to other people.
- Give feedback on the work, not on the person.
- Accept feedback from others and learn from it.
- Assume good intent, and ask a question when you do not understand.
- Respect the time of the maintainers. They are often volunteers.

## Unacceptable behavior

These behaviors are not allowed:

- Insults, personal attacks, and derogatory comments.
- Harassment of any kind, in public or in private.
- Sexual language or images, and unwanted sexual attention.
- Threats or encouragement of violence.
- Publishing the private information of another person, such as an address or an email address, without permission.
- Repeated, unwanted contact after a person asks you to stop.
- Any other conduct that a reasonable person sees as not appropriate in a professional setting.

## Where this applies

This code applies in all project spaces. These include issues, pull requests, discussions, review comments, and commit messages. It also applies when a person represents the project in public, for example in a talk or on social media.

## Responsibilities of the maintainers

The maintainers explain and enforce this code. They can edit or remove a comment, a commit, a pull request, or an issue that does not follow it. They can also lock a discussion. They can warn a person, and they can ban a person from the project for a time or for good.

A maintainer must keep the identity of the person who reports a problem private. A maintainer who has a conflict of interest with a report must not take part in the decision.

## How to report a problem

If you see or experience behavior that breaks this code, report it to the maintainers in private. Do not post the report in a public issue or pull request. Use the contact option of the maintainers on their GitHub profiles, or use the "Report a vulnerability" option on the Security tab of the repository and state that your report is about conduct.

In your report, include these items when you can:

- What happened, and where it happened.
- The links or screenshots that show it.
- The names of the people who were involved.

The maintainers will read every report. They will reply to you, and they will tell you what they decide to do when it is safe to share that.

## Enforcement steps

The maintainers use these steps. They can skip a step if the behavior is serious.

1. Private note: the maintainers tell the person which behavior broke the code and ask them to stop.
2. Warning: the maintainers give a written warning and state the consequences of more violations.
3. Temporary ban: the person cannot take part in the project for a set time.
4. Permanent ban: the person cannot take part in the project again.

## Appeals

A person who receives an enforcement action can reply to the maintainer who sent it and ask for a second review. A different maintainer will do the review when one is available.
